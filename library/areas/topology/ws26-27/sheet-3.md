### Exercise 3: $|K|$ as a subspace of $\ell^1(V;\mathbb{R})$

**Exercise 3.** Let $K=(V,\Sigma)$ be a simplicial complex and assume that $V$ is countable. Give an alternative definition of the geometric realisation $|K|$ as a subspace of $\ell^1(V;\mathbb{R})$.

*Bonus exercise:* Show that the two different geometric realisations are homeomorphic for finite simplicial complexes.
